Classic Astrological Reference

Linda Goodman's Seven Sun Sign Vibrations

In her landmark masterwork Love Signs (1978), Linda Goodman established that all 144 zodiac pairings are governed by seven universal karmic vibration patterns based on their distance around the zodiac wheel.

1-1 Vibration Same Sign (0°)

The Cosmic Mirror Alignment

You see your own reflection in your partner. This creates telepathic empathy and instant mutual understanding, though both must guard against magnifying shared faults and ego stubbornness.

Karmic Lesson: Cultivating humility through self-acceptance.

2-12 Vibration Adjacent (30°)

The Karmic Teacher & Pupil

One sign has spiritually passed through the other's grade in the cosmic school. There is deep unspoken compassion, tolerance, and secret admiration. One partner gently tutors the other.

Karmic Lesson: Bridging contrasting tempos through patient compassion.

3-11 Vibration Sextile (60°)

Karmic Friends & Sympathetic Lovers

One of the happiest vibrations in the cosmos! Signs share natural camaraderie. Even through heated quarrels, 3-11 lovers remain steadfast allies who forgive quickly and laugh easily.

Karmic Lesson: Nurturing lifelong loyalty and effortless mutual forgiveness.

4-10 Vibration Square (90°)

The Karmic Challenge & Crucible

Sharing the same modality but differing elements produces magnetic attraction alongside sharp clashes of will. If ego yields to love, this crucible tempers diamond-strength devotion.

Karmic Lesson: Transcending pride and mastering selfless compromise.

5-9 Vibration Trine (120°)

The Divine Cosmic Blessing

Linda Goodman's most celebrated synastry pattern! Sharing the same sacred element brings instant soul recognition, telepathic sympathy, and effortless encouragement of mutual dreams.

Karmic Lesson: Preserving gratitude without taking harmony for granted.

6-8 Vibration Quincunx (150°)

Karmic Mystery & Devoted Service

Cloaked in hypnotic fascination and karmic duty. One partner feels mysteriously compelled to serve, protect, or nurture the other, carrying deep emotional or physical magnetism.

Karmic Lesson: Practicing unconditional love free from selfish demands.

7-7 Vibration Opposition (180°)

Polar Magnetism & Cosmic Complements

Two halves of a single soul sitting 180° opposite on the zodiac wheel. What one lacks, the other possesses in overflowing abundance. High electric voltage: either a magnificent lifelong union or an intense clash of wills.

Karmic Lesson: Finding wholeness through merging opposite qualities into a unified yin-and-yang circle.
